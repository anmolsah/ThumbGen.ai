# Product Context (`PRODUCT.md`)

## Overview
**ThumbGen.ai** is a studio-grade AI thumbnail engineering platform for YouTube and social video creators. Unlike generic image generators that expose raw model strings or produce recognizable "AI-slop" templates, ThumbGen.ai pairs a multi-provider model registry (`openrouter` + direct `xai`) with an Anti-Slop Quality System (server-side brief compiler, code-rendered typography, safe-zone overlays, and an automated two-layer QA gate) and an atomic integer credit ledger.

## Users
1. **Independent YouTube Creators (`Starter` & `Creator` packs):** Need fast, high-contrast `16:9` (`1280×720` / `3840×2160`) and `9:16` Shorts thumbnails where the focal subject is clear at `320×180` mobile feed size and never collides with the bottom-right duration badge.
2. **Channel Producers & Thumbnail Editors (`Creator` & `Pro` packs):** Upload real subject/face reference photos, require strict likeness preservation, 4K master exports + `<= 2 MB` Studio-ready JPEG fallbacks, and predictable credit costs with zero charge on failed generations.

## Surface Modes
- **Marketing & Pricing (`/`, `/#pricing`, `/#features`):** **Persuade** mode — Asymmetric studio-instrument hero, config-derived pricing table with transparent mode credit breakdown (`1 cr` Fast, `2 cr` Standard, `3 cr` Premium, `5 cr` Ultra 4K), zero fabricated testimonials or unverified CTR statistics.
- **Thumbnail Studio & Archive (`/generate`, `/my-generation`, `/profile`):** **Operate** mode — Precision darkroom workbench (`#090a0c` canvas, `#111317` inspector panels) where the interface recedes behind the artwork, controls state exact credit costs upfront, and canvas overlays verify safe zones and mobile feed legibility.

## Brand Commitments
- **Honesty & Verifiable Claims (Rules R2, R8, Section 10A.9):** Every plan price, credit grant, and mode cost comes from `server/config/registry.json`. Never claim "undetectable as AI" or cite unverified CTR percentages.
- **Zero-Risk Credit Guarantee:** Credits are reserved atomically before queueing and refunded 100% automatically on provider or QA failure.
